How to read this assay

Side-by-side parameter comparison

ParameterA549 cancerCI-deficient fibroblastInterpretation
ECAR (extracellular acidification rate, mpH/min) reports glycolytic lactate + proton efflux; OCR (O₂ consumption rate, pmol/min) reports mitochondrial electron transport. Oligomycin inhibits ATP synthase (Complex V) — OCR collapses to the proton-leak floor and ECAR climbs as the cell shunts pyruvate to lactate. 2-deoxyglucose blocks hexokinase, starving glycolysis. Galactose medium (no glucose) forces OXPHOS via the Leloir pathway, unmasking spare respiratory capacity — or, in mitochondrial disease, revealing its absence. Parameters derived as in Agilent Seahorse Wave / XF reports.
